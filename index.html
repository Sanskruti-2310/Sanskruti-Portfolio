About

Hi, I'm Sanskruti.

I design experiences that help a business grow by genuinely connecting with its audience, without flattening the culture and values that made the brand worth connecting with in the first place. That balance is the part I find most interesting.

UX & brand designer, MSc in User Experience Design (Loughborough) on an engineering base. I'm as comfortable defending a decision with statistics as I am selling it with a story.

Four design roles in, the thing I value most isn't the toolkit, it's judgement. Tools change every season; I pick them up in days. The thinking is what transfers.
